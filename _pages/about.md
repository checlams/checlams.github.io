We are part of [School of Chemical Engineering](https://ceat.okstate.edu/che/) at [Oklahoma State University](https://go.okstate.edu/), led by [Prof. Zheyu Jiang]({{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}). At CLAMS, we develop advanced computational theories, methods, and tools, as well as systems-oriented solutions and insights to address some of the greatest challenges of our times, such as in **Industrial Decarbonization**, **Advanced Manufacturing**, **Digital Agriculture**, and **Sustainability**. To learn more about what we do, feel free to [talk to us](mailto:zheyu.jiang@okstate.edu) or come and say hi at conferences.

🚀 **CLAMS will be relocating to [University of South Florida](https://www.usf.edu/) in Tampa, Florida starting Spring 2027 (Go Pokes! → Go Bulls!). We are excited for this transition, and we have one to two fully-funded PhD opening for Fall 2027.** Please [check here]({{ '/assets/pdf/opening_27.pdf' | relative_url }}) for description of the position and instructions to apply.
